## Internal workflow

`dissent-kit-internal-workflow.jpg` explains the five mappings and the Deliberation sequence:

- Rook to Direct
- Bishop to Strategy
- Knight to Blind spot
- Queen to Synthesis
- King to Stakes

The horizontal line through the pieces shows that evidence applies to every lens. The lower pathway moves from framing to pre-mortem, challenge, verdict, and falsifier.

Both images were created for DissentKit with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 25 August 2026. They use optimized JPEG encoding to keep repository downloads small.
