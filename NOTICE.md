# Provenance and notices

DissentKit was created by combining and adapting two earlier skills supplied by the project author.

## Reality-First Critic

Reality-First Critic provides the core discipline behind Direct Review: verdict first, no opening flattery, fair representation of the user's intent, explicit confidence, resistance to pressure without new evidence, and a correction after the critique.

The supplied source declares the MIT license. Its header does not identify a copyright holder or canonical public repository, so this notice does not invent one.

## Council

Council provides the deeper decision process adapted for Deliberation: pre-mortem before commitment, anonymous challenge, confidence movement, visible disagreement, a falsifier, and a later review point.

The supplied source declares the MIT license. It also credits the general persona set in `0xNyk/council-of-high-intelligence`, MIT license, copyright 2026 nyk. DissentKit does not copy that persona set. The reference remains relevant to the history of the Council skill:

<https://github.com/0xNyk/council-of-high-intelligence>

## What DissentKit adds

DissentKit does not claim the underlying review methods as original. This repository adds a two-level routing model, a compact chess-derived lens system with functional names, explicit labels for single-context and isolated execution, cross-platform adapters, evaluation fixtures, examples, and project artwork.

All rewritten DissentKit files are distributed under the repository's MIT license. Upstream notices remain with their respective projects.
